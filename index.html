Four minutes on the box at the subway-station party. Hit every prompt, keep the crowd hyped.

Arrows / WASD or D-pad to move · Enter or (A) to select · ←/→ change options

How to play

Keyboard

←↑↓→ or AWSD

Esc pause

Controller

D-pad, left stick, or face buttons laid out like the arrows: X left, Y up, A down, B right

Start pause

Prompts scroll along the platform edge toward the target on the left. Press as each one crosses the ring. Long bars are holds: keep the button down until the tail passes. Miss one and the dancer stumbles and the music glitches. Keep your combo going to raise the score multiplier and the crowd's hype.

If hits feel early or late, nudge Audio offset on the title screen. Positive values move the timing later.
